US Zakat Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

In short: US Zakat Calculator runs entirely in your browser. The financial information you enter is stored on your own device, and — only if you choose — in your own Google Drive. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we never see, sell, or share it.

This policy explains how US Zakat Calculator (“the app”, “we”), available at https://uszakat.sspods.com/, handles information. The app is a free, open-source tool that helps US-based Muslims calculate zakat on income, investment, and retirement accounts.

Information you enter

To calculate zakat, you may enter account names, account types, balances, liabilities, settings (such as your hawl date and calculation method), and records of past zakat reviews and payments. The app does not ask for your name, email address, account numbers, or login credentials for any financial institution.

Where your data is stored

  • In your browser. Your data is saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. It stays there until you clear it. Anyone with access to your browser profile can see it.
  • In a file you choose (optional). You can export a backup file, or — in supported browsers — link a data file on your computer that the app updates as you work. That file is saved wherever you put it (for example, a folder synced by iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive, in which case that service’s own privacy terms apply).
  • In your Google Drive (optional). See the next section.

Your financial data is never sent to servers operated by us. We have no database and no user accounts.

Google user data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. When you click “Google Drive” under Back Up & Sync, Google asks you to grant the app the following permissions:

  • drive.appdata — to store a backup of your zakat data in a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive. The app can access only the files it created in that folder; it cannot see your other Drive files.
  • drive.file — to save Excel zakat reports to a Drive folder that you choose with Google’s file picker. The app can access only files it created or that you explicitly select.

The app does not request access to your Google profile, email address, or contacts.

How we access and use Google user data. The app uses this access only to:

  • save a backup file (us-zakat-calculator-backup.json) containing the zakat data you entered, and update it as you make changes;
  • read that backup file to restore your data on another browser or device, or after your browser data is cleared;
  • check when the backup was last modified, so the app can warn you before overwriting newer data;
  • upload Excel reports you ask it to save to a Drive folder.

How Google user data is stored and transferred. All Google Drive requests are made directly from your browser to Google’s servers over HTTPS. The temporary access token Google issues is kept in your browser’s session storage and is discarded when you close the tab or disconnect. Data read from your Drive is stored only in your browser’s local storage, as described above.

Sharing. We do not share, sell, or transfer Google user data to anyone. No person, including the developer, reads your Google user data. We do not use it for advertising, for determining creditworthiness or lending purposes, or for any purpose other than the features described above.

AI and machine learning. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.

Limited Use. US Zakat Calculator’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion. The backup stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. You can:

  • choose Disconnect Drive in the app to stop syncing and revoke the app’s access token;
  • remove the app’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions page;
  • delete the backup by opening Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, finding US Zakat Calculator, and choosing Delete hidden app data;
  • delete any Excel reports from your Drive like any other file.

Other services the app contacts

  • Gold price. To compute the nisab threshold, your browser requests the current gold price from Gold-API.com. No personal or financial data is included in this request, though, like any website, that service can see your IP address.
  • Google sign-in scripts. When you connect Google Drive, your browser loads Google’s sign-in and file picker scripts from Google, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting, security, and site statistics. The app is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request information (such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse:
    • Security. Cloudflare may run a bot-detection check in your browser and set a security cookie (cf_clearance) used only to tell people apart from automated traffic.
    • Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused service that does not use cookies or fingerprinting, to see aggregate statistics such as the number of visits, pages viewed, referring sites, browser and device types, countries, and page load speed.
    These statistics do not identify you to us and never include the financial data you enter or any Google user data.

The app does not include advertising, and we do not sell or share information about your visits.

Your choices

  • Export or import your data at any time from Settings → Your Data.
  • Delete your data by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, deleting any data files you created, and deleting your Drive backup as described above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version on this page. The source code and the history of changes to this policy are public on GitHub.
