US Zakat Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy applies to US Zakat Calculator (“the app”, “we”, “us”), a free web application at https://uszakat.sspods.com/ developed by Sayyid Sofwan. The app helps Muslims in the United States calculate the zakat they owe on cash, investment, and retirement accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it uses, stores, shares, and protects that data, and how you can delete it — including data the app accesses from your Google Account through Google APIs.

In short: The app runs in your browser. The financial information you enter is stored on your own device and, only if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not run a server or database that receives your financial or Google data, we do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Data the app accesses

Information you provide. To calculate zakat, you may enter account names and types, asset balances, liabilities, settings (such as your hawl date and calculation method), and records of past zakat reviews and payments. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, financial account numbers, or financial login credentials.

Google user data (only if you connect Google Drive). Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you choose to connect it, the app asks for these Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — access to a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive. The app accesses only the backup file it creates there (us-zakat-calculator-backup.json), which contains the zakat data you entered, and that file’s metadata (such as its last-modified time).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — access only to files the app creates, or that you explicitly select with Google’s file picker. The app uses it to create Excel zakat reports in a Drive folder you choose, and to read that folder’s name and ID.

The app cannot see, read, or change any other files in your Google Drive. It does not request access to your Google profile, name, email address, contacts, or any other Google service.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives standard request information such as your IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and the pages you request. See section 7.

2. How the app uses data

The app uses the information you provide only to calculate zakat and show your results, history, and payment tracking to you.

The app uses Google user data only to provide these features that you turn on and can see in the app:

  • Backup and restore: saving your zakat data to the backup file in your Drive’s app folder whenever it changes, and reading it back to restore your data on another browser or device, or after your browser data is cleared;
  • Sync safety: checking the backup’s last-modified time so the app can warn you before overwriting newer data;
  • Report export: saving Excel zakat reports you ask for to the Drive folder you choose.

We do not use Google user data for any other purpose. In particular, we do not use or transfer Google user data:

  • to serve advertisements, including targeted, personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising;
  • to sell to, or share with, data brokers or information resellers;
  • to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • to build databases or profiles about users;
  • to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models. Data obtained through Google Workspace APIs, including the Google Drive API, is not used to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI and/or ML models.

No person, including the developer, reads your Google user data.

3. How data is shared

We do not sell, rent, transfer, or disclose your information — including Google user data — to third parties. Your financial and Google data is never sent to us: all Google Drive requests go directly from your browser to Google’s servers. The only exceptions would be if you ask us to (for example, by sending us a file yourself), or if required by law.

4. Where data is stored

  • Your browser: the data you enter is saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. Data read from your Google Drive backup is stored only here as well.
  • A file you choose (optional): you can export a backup file or link a data file on your computer. It is saved wherever you put it; if that is a cloud-synced folder (such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive), that provider’s privacy terms also apply.
  • Your Google Drive (optional): the backup file and any Excel reports are stored in your own Google Drive, under your Google Account.

We do not operate any server or database that stores your financial information or Google user data.

5. How data is protected

  • The site and all communication with Google’s APIs use HTTPS (TLS encryption) in transit.
  • Because your data is not sent to our servers, there is no central copy of user data that could be breached.
  • The app requests only the narrowest Google Drive scopes needed (drive.appdata and drive.file), so it cannot access the rest of your Drive.
  • The temporary access token Google issues is kept only in your browser’s session storage. It is discarded when you close the tab, and revoked when you choose Disconnect Drive.
  • Data in your Google Drive is protected by Google’s security, and data in your browser by your device and browser. Anyone with access to your browser profile can see the data stored there, so protect your device accordingly.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as you keep it; we have no copy to retain.

  • Browser data stays until you delete it. Clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove it.
  • Files you export or link stay until you delete them.
  • Google Drive data: the backup stays in your Drive until you delete it. To stop access, choose Disconnect Drive in the app or remove US Zakat Calculator from your Google Account permissions. To delete the backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find US Zakat Calculator, and choose Delete hidden app data. Excel reports can be deleted like any other Drive file.

Because we do not hold your data, we cannot delete it for you, but we are happy to help you through these steps — see Contact below.

7. Other services the app uses

  • Google: when you connect Google Drive, your browser loads Google’s sign-in and file picker scripts, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Gold price: to compute the nisab threshold, your browser requests the current gold price from Gold-API.com. No personal or financial data is included, though that service can see your IP address.
  • Cloudflare (hosting, security, and statistics): the site is served by Cloudflare, which processes request information to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Cloudflare may run a bot-detection check in your browser and set a security cookie (cf_clearance) used only to tell people apart from automated traffic. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or fingerprinting, to see aggregate statistics such as visits, pages viewed, referring sites, browser and device types, countries, and page load speed. These statistics do not identify you to us and never include the financial data you enter or any Google user data.

The app does not show advertising.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

US Zakat Calculator’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app accesses, uses, stores, or shares data, we will update this policy, change the date above, and show a notice in the app before the change takes effect. The history of this policy is public on GitHub.

11. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your data, open an issue at github.com/sysofwan/USZakat/issues.
