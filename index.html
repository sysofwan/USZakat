What it does

Everything you need for an accurate zakat calculation

Built for the way Muslims in the US actually hold wealth — across banks, brokerages, and retirement plans.

Every account in one place

Bank, brokerage, 401(k), IRA, HSA, and debt accounts, organized the way you hold them.

Retirement accounts done right

Long-term or short-term methods for 401(k)s and IRAs, including taxes and early-withdrawal penalties.

Stocks and funds

Applies a zakatable-asset percentage to passively held stocks and funds — per fund or as a default.

Live nisab check

Compares your wealth against the nisab threshold using the current price of gold.

Guided annual review

A step-by-step zakat review each year on your hawl (lunar anniversary) date.

History and payments

Keep past reviews, track zakat payments as you make them, and export reports to Excel.

How it works

Three steps, once a year

1

Add your accounts

List your bank, brokerage, retirement, and debt accounts. No account numbers or logins needed.

2

Run your annual review

On your hawl date, enter balances. The app applies FCNA methods, stock proxies, and the nisab check.

3

Pay and keep records

See exactly what you owe, log payments as you make them, and export a report.

Privacy

Your data and Google Drive

Your zakat data stays with you. There’s no account to create and no server that stores your finances.

Saved on your device

Your financial information is saved in your browser and is never sent to our servers.

Backup files

To keep it safe or use it on another device, download a backup file, or link a data file on your computer that updates as you work.

Why the app asks for Google Drive access

Optional. If you connect it, the app uses Drive only to back up your zakat data to a hidden, app-only folder and to save Excel reports to a folder you pick. It can’t see the rest of your Drive, and never shares or sells your data.
